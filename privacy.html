AIStack Pocket Relay

Private by design

This host serves the Pocket web app and forwards only bounded, end-to-end encrypted pairing frames between a paired phone and AIStack Studio.

The relay does not execute AI tasks, decrypt or retain task text, results, files, pairing material, provider credentials, accounts, analytics, or a database.

AIStack Studio remains the execution authority. It connects outbound to the relay; this service does not expose a desktop or dispatcher endpoint.
